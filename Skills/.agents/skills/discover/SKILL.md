Read and follow `SE-Workflow/1)Problem.md` completely.

The user has a new idea. Your job is to grill them relentlessly until the business problem is crystal clear, build domain language in GLOSSARY.md, record hard-to-reverse decisions as ADRs, and produce `problem-overview.md`.

Do not skip steps. Do not assume answers. Facts are your job; decisions are the user's.
